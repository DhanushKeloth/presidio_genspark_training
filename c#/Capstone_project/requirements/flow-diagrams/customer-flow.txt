For your **FastShip Logistics Platform**, the workflows should be organized by role. These are the exact flows I'd use for your prototype and project presentation.

# 1. CUSTOMER WORKFLOW

## A. New Customer Registration

```text id="lcbnjk"
Landing Page
      ↓
Login
      ↓
Register
      ↓
Account Type Selection
      ↓
Customer Registration
      ↓
Customer Dashboard
```

---

## B. Book Shipment Workflow

```text id="lq2yq7"
Customer Dashboard
      ↓
Book Shipment
      ↓
Booking Success Modal
      ↓
Shipment Detail
```

### Actions

* Enter pickup details
* Enter recipient details
* Add package information
* Confirm booking

Result:

```text id="xz5mjd"
Shipment Created
Tracking Number Generated
```

---
